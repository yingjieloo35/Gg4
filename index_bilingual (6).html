Introduction游戏介绍 What is Honor of Kings: World? 《王者荣耀世界》是什么游戏？

Honor of Kings: World is an open-world action RPG set in the vast continent of Jixia (稷下大陆). You play as the Origin Flow Child (元流之子) — a customisable avatar — who resonates with legendary heroes to fight, explore, and uncover the game's epic story.

Developed by TiMi Studio Group (Tencent), it launched on PC in China on April 10, 2026. The game shares heroes with the original Honor of Kings MOBA (like Xi Shi, Kaiser, Garo) but is completely standalone — no prior knowledge needed.

The main story runs 40–60 hours. With side content and exploration, expect well over 100 hours.

《王者荣耀世界》是一款以稷下大陆为舞台的开放世界动作RPG。玩家扮演可自定义的元流之子，通过与各路英雄产生"共鸣"，一同战斗、探索，揭开宏大的世界故事。

游戏由腾讯天美工作室研发，于2026年4月10日率先登陆PC端。游戏中包含西施、铠、伽罗等王者荣耀IP英雄，但本作是完全独立的新游戏，无需了解原版手游即可上手。

主线剧情约需40–60小时，加上支线与探索内容，预计超过100小时游戏体验。

⚔️

Genre游戏类型

Open-world Action RPG with real-time combo-based combat and seamless exploration.开放世界动作RPG，实时连招战斗，无缝大地图探索。

👥

Solo or Co-op单人/多人

Full story completable solo. Co-op with up to 3 other players is optional for bosses and dungeons.主线可完全单人通关。BOSS战和副本支持最多4人联机，多人可选。

Platform游戏平台

Currently PC. Mobile version planned. Global release TBA.目前仅PC端。手游版本在规划中。国服已上线，全球版发布时间待定。

💡 "Is this like Genshin Impact?" — Structurally similar: a team of three, open world, boss fights. The difference is faster action-focused combat and a Chinese imperial fantasy setting. If you've played Genshin, you'll adapt quickly. 💡 "这游戏像原神吗？" — 结构上有相似之处：三人小队、开放世界、BOSS战。区别在于战斗节奏更快、动作性更强，且以中国古代奇幻为主题背景。玩过原神的玩家会很快上手。

Getting Started开局指引 What to Do in Your First Hour 开局第一小时该做什么

When you first load in, it can feel overwhelming. Follow this exact order:

刚进游戏可能会感到不知所措。按以下顺序来做，保证不走弯路：

  1. Follow the tutorial and main story (觉醒任务 / Awakening Quests).跟着主线任务走（觉醒任务）。 The game guides you through the basics in the first 20–30 minutes. Don't skip dialogue — it explains the world and unlocks core systems.游戏会在前20–30分钟通过主线引导你了解基本玩法。建议不要跳过对话——它会解锁核心系统并介绍世界观。
  2. Learn to dodge first.先学会闪避。 Press Shift or Right Click. This single mechanic saves your life more than anything else.按Shift或鼠标右键闪避。这是全游戏最重要的操作，没有之一。
  3. Activate every teleport point (传送锚点) you see.激活沿途所有传送锚点。 Glowing beacons throughout the world. Interact to unlock instant fast-travel. Your top priority in every new zone.世界中发光的信标即为传送锚点，互动即可解锁快速传送。进入新区域时第一件事就是找锚点激活。
  4. Pick up every resource node.沿途收集所有资源节点。 Herbs, food, and materials are scattered everywhere. Grab them constantly — they become potions and upgrade materials.草药、食材、材料散布全图，随手收集。这些会变成战斗回血的药品和后期升级材料。
  5. Complete Daily Missions (日常任务) every day.每天完成日常任务。 Once unlocked, these are your most efficient source of EXP and currency. Make them a daily habit.解锁后，日常任务是效率最高的经验和货币来源。每次登录后优先完成。
  6. Don't ignore Resonance Quests (共鸣任务).不要忽视共鸣任务。 Character-specific quests that give large one-time EXP and strengthen your heroes.英雄专属任务，提供大量一次性经验奖励，同时强化对应英雄。出现即做。
💡 #1 beginner mistake: Getting distracted by the open world and ignoring the main story. Explore freely, but keep returning to story quests — they unlock fast travel points, characters, and critical systems. 💡 新手最常犯的错误：沉迷于自由探索而忽略主线任务。可以随意溜达，但要记得回来推主线——主线会解锁传送点、英雄和关键系统。

Core Mechanics核心机制 How Combat Works 战斗系统详解

Combat is real-time. You control one character from your team, swapping between them with Tab. Basic attacks generate Flow Energy (源流) which powers your skills.

战斗为实时动作制。你控制队伍中的一名角色，按Tab切换。普通攻击会积累源流能量，用于释放技能。

Skill Energy技能能量

Skills Q/E/R require Flow Energy built by attacking. Think of basic attacks as fuel — keep attacking to keep your skills available.Q/E/R技能需要消耗源流能量，通过普攻积累。把普攻理解为燃料——持续输出才能保持技能可用。

Perfect Dodge (完美闪避)完美闪避

Dodge at the exact moment of impact to trigger Perfect Dodge — time slows and opens an Enhanced Attack Window. Press E immediately for a bonus hit.在敌人攻击命中瞬间闪避，触发完美闪避——时间短暂减速并开启强化攻击窗口。立刻按E打出额外伤害。

① Attack普攻

Build Flow Energy with basic attacks.通过普攻积累源流能量。

② Use Skills释放技能

Fire Q/E/R as they charge.能量充足时释放Q/E/R技能。

③ Dodge闪避

Read enemy animations, dodge before impact.观察敌人动作，在攻击前闪避。

④ Ultimate大招

Save P for Broken State.大招留到BOSS虚弱期释放。

🥷 Core philosophy: "Dodge first, punish second." Never stand still trading hits. Move constantly, dodge away when in trouble, then re-engage. 🥷 战斗核心理念："先闪后打"。永远不要站桩硬扛。持续走位，危险时先闪开，再重新输出。
🔼 Mid-air attacks: Attacking while jumping triggers unique bonus effects per character. Experiment to discover your hero's aerial combos. 🔼 空中攻击：跳跃时攻击会触发各英雄独特的空中连招效果，多多尝试你的英雄的空中招式。

Interface游戏界面 Reading the HUD — What All Those Bars Mean 界面解读——各种血条是什么意思

There are three separate health indicators — this confuses almost every new player:

游戏共有三个独立的生命值指示器，几乎每个新手都会搞混：

Top bar上方血条
Active character's HP. Depletes when your current hero takes hits.当前操控角色的生命值，受击时减少。
Bottom bar下方血条
Backline characters' combined HP. They take reduced damage when inactive.后排英雄的综合生命值。待机状态受到的伤害会减少。
Blue circle蓝色圆圈
CORE HP核心血量
Source Flow Core (源流核心) — you only truly lose when this hits zero. Individual character deaths chip away at it but it doesn't empty instantly.源流核心——只有这个归零才算真正失败。单个角色阵亡只会消耗一部分，不会立即清空。
💡 Low on HP? Press Tab to swap to a teammate immediately. Your wounded character moves to the backline and takes reduced damage while you fight with someone else. 💡 血量告急？立刻按Tab切换队友上场。受伤的角色退到后排，受到伤害减少，给你恢复的时间。

Unique Mechanic特色机制 The Primal Flow System (源流之环) 源流之环——元流之子的核心能力

Your Avatar (元流之子) is not locked to one fighting style. Using the Primal Flow System (源流之环), you can switch between weapon forms mid-combat by spending Flow Energy. Each form changes your attacks, skills, and playstyle entirely. In S1, you can have two styles active at once.

元流之子不受限于单一战斗风格。通过源流之环系统，消耗源流能量即可在战斗中切换武器形态。每种形态的攻击、技能和打法都截然不同。S1赛季后，可同时装备两个流派自由切换。

⚪

Ring Blades (环刃)环刃

Fast spinning close-range attacks. High burst damage. Best for aggressive players.高速旋转的近战形态，爆发伤害强。适合喜欢激进打法的玩家。

🏹

Lance (长枪)长枪

Long reach thrusting attacks. Good for keeping distance from dangerous enemies.攻击距离长，突刺为主。适合与危险敌人保持距离的打法。

🔨

Hammer (重锤)重锤

Heavy slow swings. Excellent for breaking enemy Toughness bars and staggering bosses.沉重缓慢的挥击，破韧效果极佳，是打断BOSS白条的首选。

⚔️

Sword (长剑)长剑

Balanced all-rounder. Good damage with decent speed. Best default for beginners.均衡型武器，伤害与速度兼顾。新手首选，适应各种场景。

💡 For beginners: Pick Sword or Ring Blades and stick with it while learning the basics. Don't overthink weapon swapping early on — master one form first. 💡 新手建议：初期选择长剑或环刃，专注练好基本功。不用急着研究切换流派——先把一个形态用熟再说。

Combat Depth进阶战斗 Boss Fights — How to Win Them BOSS战——如何稳定通关

  1. Red glow = dodge immediately.红色光效 = 立刻闪避。 Bosses glow red before powerful attacks. Stop attacking and dodge away now. These can kill you in one or two hits if ignored.BOSS发出红色光效代表即将释放强力技能。立刻停止攻击并闪避。不躲的话一两刀就能带走你。
  2. Deplete the white Toughness bar (韧性条).打空白色韧性条。 Every boss has a white bar below their HP. Keep attacking — Hammer form breaks it fastest. When empty, the boss enters Broken State (虚弱状态).每个BOSS血条下方有一条白色韧性条。持续输出即可打断——重锤形态破韧最快。韧性条清空后BOSS进入虚弱状态。
  3. Broken State = your big damage window.虚弱状态 = 爆发输出时机。 Boss is stunned. All damage increased. Press X for a finisher. Use your Ultimate (P) right now for maximum damage.BOSS进入眩晕，全队伤害提升。按X释放处决技能，同时立刻使用大招（P），这是伤害最高的时机。
  4. Boss shields block your moves.BOSS护盾会封锁你的技能。 When shielded, your red-glow skills are blocked. Focus on breaking the shield first, then resume damage.BOSS开护盾时你的红光技能会被封锁。先集中破盾，再恢复正常输出。
  5. Swap characters when in trouble.危险时切换角色。 If your active character is near death, press Tab immediately. Never just stand there hoping to survive.当前角色快死时立刻按Tab换人。不要傻站着硬扛，你有队伍，充分利用。
⚠️ Most common mistake: Using your Ultimate (P) as soon as it charges. Save it for Broken State — that's when it deals maximum damage. ⚠️ 最常见错误：大招一充好就立刻放。正确做法是留到BOSS进入虚弱状态再打——那才是伤害最大化的时机。

You build a team of two Resonance heroes (共鸣) alongside your Avatar. In combat, switch between them with Tab. Each fills a role — you need the right mix, not three of the same type.

除元流之子外，你还可以选择两名共鸣英雄组成三人小队。战斗中按Tab切换。每个英雄有专属定位——需要合理搭配，而非堆叠同一类型。

Stand at the front, absorb damage, keep enemies focused while your DPS attacks safely. Tough and hard to kill — and they deal real damage too.站在最前方承受伤害，为后排输出创造安全空间。耐打，且自身也有可观的伤害输出。

《王者荣耀世界》采用双等级系统：

Your main power level. Unlocks character upgrades, weapon enhancements, and new systems. Always try to raise this.你的主要战力等级，解锁英雄养成、武器强化和新系统。这是你一直要提升的核心数值。

The world difficulty tier. Acts as a cap on your Source Flow Level. Raised by pushing the main story (境界突破任务).世界难度层级，同时也是元流等级的上限。通过完成主线境界突破任务来提升。

Daily Routine每日习惯 Stamina & What to Do Each Day 体力系统与每日必做事项

Stamina (体力) regenerates over time (~160/day). It's spent on Flow Trial dungeons (源流试炼) that drop upgrade materials. Don't hoard it — stamina has a cap, so unused regen is wasted.

体力随时间自动恢复（约每日160点），用于挑战源流试炼副本以获取养成材料。不要囤积体力——体力有上限，溢出后的恢复量会被白白浪费。

  1. Complete all Daily Missions (日常任务).完成所有日常任务。 Reset every day. Biggest EXP + currency source per session. Do these first, every login.每日重置。是每次登录中经验和货币效率最高的来源。每次登录第一件事。
  2. Spend stamina on Flow Trial dungeons (源流试炼).把体力用在源流试炼上。 Run until stamina is at a comfortable low. Don't save it.刷到体力接近耗尽为止。不要存体力。
  3. Kill new Elite Bosses (精英怪) for free.免费击杀新遇到的精英怪。 First kills cost no stamina and give good rewards. Always clear new elites when entering a new region.首杀不消耗体力，奖励丰厚。进入新区域时务必先把精英怪各打一遍。
  4. Push the main story.推进主线任务。 Even one quest per session keeps you moving forward.每次游戏至少完成一个主线任务，保持稳步推进。
  5. Collect resource nodes while travelling.移动途中顺手采集资源。 They respawn daily. These add up to hundreds of potions over time.资源节点每日刷新，日积月累能攒出大量回血道具。
⚠️ Stamina cap warning: If you don't log in for several days and stamina is already full, new regeneration is wasted. Log in daily even briefly. ⚠️ 体力上限提示：若几天不登录且体力已满，新恢复的体力会溢出丢失。每天哪怕短暂登录消耗一下体力，收益也不会白费。

Open World开放世界 Exploration Tips That Save You Hours 大地图探索技巧

HoK: World has no traditional minimap. Navigation uses a Directional Compass Line at the top of the screen pointing toward your objective. It feels unusual for 30 minutes, then becomes natural.

《王者荣耀世界》没有传统小地图。导航依靠屏幕上方的方向指引线指向当前目标。前30分钟会有些不习惯，之后就会成为本能。

  1. Activate teleport points (传送锚点) immediately.立刻激活传送锚点。 Walk up and interact. Unlocks instant fast-travel from anywhere. Never skip one.靠近互动即可解锁，之后可从任意地点瞬间传送至此。遇到绝对不能错过。
  2. Pick up every resource node.顺手采集所有资源节点。 Reset daily. Becomes hundreds of potions over time.每日刷新，坚持采集能积累大量回血药材。
  3. Do Kunyou instances (坤舆副本) when they appear.遇到坤舆副本优先进入。 Special story scenarios that give high EXP and Resonance materials. Higher priority than general wandering.专属剧情副本，经验和共鸣材料丰厚。优先级高于普通探索。
  4. Use the skateboard (滑板) for fast travel.善用滑板快速移动。 Summon for fast movement across flat terrain. Supports instant switch to combat mode.平地移动快速，支持无缝切入战斗状态，大幅缩短探索时间。
  5. Use grapple hook and gliding.合理使用钩锁和滑翔。 Grapple hook to climb buildings and trees. Hold jump at height to glide. Essential for vertical exploration.钩锁可快速攀爬建筑和树木；高处长按跳跃键可滑翔。竖向探索必备技能。

Questions常见问题 FAQ — Things New Players Always Ask 新手最常问的问题

Do I need to play the original Honor of Kings first? 需要先玩过原版王者荣耀吗？ ▾
Not at all. HoK: World is a completely standalone game. The only connection to the original MOBA is that some heroes appear in both — but their stories and mechanics are entirely different here. 完全不需要。《王者荣耀世界》是独立游戏。与原版手游的唯一联系是部分英雄两款游戏都有，但剧情和玩法完全不同，无需任何原版游戏经验。
Is there a gacha system? Can I lose characters to bad luck? 游戏有抽卡系统吗？英雄会不会靠运气才能拿到？ ▾
No character gacha. All heroes are earned through story, events, Battle Pass (战令), or bought directly with Jing Po (晶珀) at a fixed price. The only gacha mechanic (Heavenly Reward / 天降礼遇) is for cosmetic skins only. 英雄没有抽卡系统。所有英雄通过主线任务、活动、战令获取，或使用晶珀固定价格直接购买。唯一的抽卡机制（天降礼遇）仅限皮肤外观，与战斗力无关。
I keep dying. What am I doing wrong? 我一直死，到底哪里操作有问题？ ▾
Almost always one of these: (1) Not dodging — press Shift before enemy attacks land. (2) Ignoring red telegraphs — bosses glow red before big attacks; dodge immediately. (3) Not swapping characters — press Tab when your hero is low on HP. (4) Wasting Ultimate — save P for Broken State. 基本上是这几个原因：①没有闪避——敌人攻击前按Shift。②忽视红光预警——BOSS亮红光时立刻闪开。③不切角色——血量危险时按Tab换人。④大招时机错误——把P留到BOSS虚弱期再放。
What is the Primal Flow System (源流之环)? 源流之环是什么？ ▾
Your Avatar (元流之子) can switch between weapon combat styles — Ring Blades, Lance, Hammer, Sword — mid-fight by spending Flow Energy. In Season 1, you can have two styles active simultaneously and swap between them freely. 元流之子通过消耗源流能量，在战斗中切换不同武器形态（环刃、长枪、重锤、长剑），每种形态攻击方式完全不同。S1赛季后可同时装备两个流派，战斗中自由切换。
How do I get more heroes? 怎么获取更多英雄？ ▾
Via: (1) main story progression, (2) limited-time events, (3) Battle Pass (战令) — e.g. Garo (伽罗) at tier 68, (4) direct purchase with Jing Po (晶珀) at a fixed price. No random elements. 通过：①主线任务推进；②限时活动；③战令——例如伽罗在第68档；④使用晶珀固定价格直接购买。没有随机成分，目标明确可达。
Can I play solo? Is multiplayer required? 可以单人游玩吗？必须联机吗？ ▾
You can complete the entire main story and most content completely solo. Co-op (up to 4 players) is available for boss fights and dungeons but is entirely optional. 主线和大部分内容均可完全单人通关。BOSS战和副本支持最多4人联机，但完全可选，不影响正常游戏体验。
How long is the game? 游戏有多长？ ▾
Main story: approximately 40–60 hours. With side content, exploration, and endgame: well over 100 hours. 主线剧情约40–60小时。加上支线任务、大地图探索和终局内容，预计超过100小时游戏时长。
What should I spend my stamina (体力) on? 体力应该用在哪里？ ▾
Spend stamina on Flow Trial dungeons (源流试炼) — they drop character upgrade materials. Don't hoard it; stamina has a cap and overflow is wasted. Elite boss first kills are free — always clear new elites before farming with stamina. 体力优先用于源流试炼副本——产出英雄养成材料。不要囤积，溢出白费。精英怪的首杀不消耗体力，进新区域时先把精英怪各打一次，再用体力刷副本。
