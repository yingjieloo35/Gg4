Introduction What is Honor of Kings: World?

Honor of Kings: World is a full-scale open-world action RPG developed by TiMi Studio Group, set in the same universe as the original Honor of Kings MOBA. Don't let the franchise fool you — this is not a mobile spin-off. It's a massive, standalone experience with deep progression systems, combo-based combat, and a story-driven world you can sink 100+ hours into.

You do not need to have played the original Honor of Kings to enjoy World. It has its own story, its own mechanics, and its own cast of characters. The main story alone runs 40–60 hours; with side content, expect well over 100 hours of play.

Progression Leveling & Progression System

HoK: World uses a dual-level structure that new players often find confusing at first. Understanding both is critical to progressing efficiently from day one.

Source Flow Level

Your primary character power level. Directly controls character ascension, weapon upgrades, rune slots, and system unlocks.

World Flow Level

The world progression tier. It caps your Source Flow Level and controls the rune level cap, plus unlocks world systems and reward tiers.

How to gain EXP efficiently:

EXP Source Daily Yield Priority
Daily Missions ~3,000 EXP (~1.5 levels early) 🔥 Highest
Main Story / Awakening Quests Variable (large chunks) 🔥 Highest
Resonance Quests (per character) Large one-time rewards ⬆ High
Flow Trials (stamina dungeons) 40 stamina ≈ 200 EXP (160 stamina/day regen) ⬆ High
First-time Elite Boss Kills No stamina cost, high yield ✅ Do once
Open World Exploration One-time per region ✅ Passive
💡 Beginner tip: Daily Missions are your bread and butter. Always complete them before anything else. They also drop currency for shop-based progression materials.
03

Core Mechanics Combat System

Combat is built around a hybrid action + stamina + stagger + dodge counter system. Each character has 3 active skills (Q / E / R) plus one Ultimate (P). Skills require combo-based energy charging — your basic attacks and skill hits build up the energy needed to chain abilities.

Basic Controls

Left Click — Basic attack
Hold — Charged attack
Shift / Right Click — Dodge
Tab — Switch character

Dodge Counter Window

Triggering Dodge just before a hit lands grants an Enhanced Attack Window — triggering E right after increases your overall damage output significantly.

Mid-Air Variants

Attacking while airborne triggers variant effects depending on each character's Resonance type. Experiment to discover your character's aerial combos.

⚔️ Combat philosophy: Dodge first, punish second. Never overcommit to attacks unless the boss is staggered or broken. Patience wins fights.
04

Advanced Combat Boss Mechanics

Bosses are where HoK: World's combat depth really shines. Learning boss patterns early will save you enormous frustration as you progress into harder content.

  1. Red telegraph = danger: Bosses show a red glowing indicator before major attacks. These can be interrupted with specific skills, dodged via perfect evade, or cancelled with ultimates.
  2. Toughness bar (white): Every boss has a white toughness bar. Deplete it to trigger the Broken State — all damage increases and you may press X for a finisher.
  3. Boss shields: When a boss has a shield active, it restricts your red-glow skills and alters the combat tempo. Focus on shield-breaking skills first.
  4. Second damage window: Successfully breaking a boss's major move stuns it briefly — this is your highest DPS window. Save burst skills for this moment.
⚠️ Common mistake: Many beginners burn their ultimate as soon as it's ready. Save ultimates for when the boss enters Broken State to maximize your damage in that critical window.

Roster Characters & Roles

Characters in HoK: World are divided into three core roles. Team building is based on functional synergy, not fixed class slots — you have flexibility in how you fill each position.

🛡 Frontline

Absorb damage, initiate fights, and handle boss mechanics. These are not pure tanks — they contribute damage while surviving frontline pressure.

Kai Hua Mulan Leng Chun
⚡ Main DPS

The damage engines of your team. They determine your overall DPS ceiling and require stable conditions to perform — protect them with your frontline and support.

Dongfang Yao Meng Ya Jia Luo
✨ Support

Control battlefield tempo through buffs, crowd control, shielding, or sustain. Makes the entire combat system run smoothly. Not focused on raw damage.

Best starter character: Lu Bu is the easiest to pick up for absolute beginners. Zhao Yun offers balanced gameplay for most players. Hou Yi is ideal if you prefer ranged combat.

Open World Exploration Tips

HoK: World has no traditional mini-map. Navigation is done via a Directional Compass Line System — align the guidance line at the top of your screen and move toward your target direction. It sounds unusual but becomes second nature quickly.

  1. Unlock teleport points immediately. Every time you reach a new area, find and activate the teleport point first. This dramatically reduces travel costs throughout the entire game.
  2. Always gather wild resources. Potions and food items found in the open world are essential for combat sustain, especially in early game boss fights.
  3. Do Kunyou instances early. Kunyou is an independent story instance that provides experience and Resonance rewards — these are some of the best early rewards in the game.
  4. Use the skateboard system. The game includes a fast terrain traversal system that supports quick switching between modes. Master it early to save significant travel time.
  5. Track exploration per region. The "Wandering Journey" interface shows your exploration progress for each area separately. Each region has unique rewards for completion milestones.
💡 Pro tip: First-time Elite Boss kills give you both experience and materials with no stamina cost. Clear every elite boss in each region before you start farming for materials with stamina.
08

Action Plan What to Do First — Early Game Priority

New players are often overwhelmed by the sheer number of systems unlocking in the first few hours. Here's the exact order to tackle things:

  1. Complete daily missions every day. This is your most efficient EXP source and provides shop currency. Non-negotiable.
  2. Push the main story (Awakening Quests). Story quests unlock systems, teleport points, and reward huge amounts of EXP. Do not ignore them to go exploring early.
  3. Do Resonance Quests for your main character. These character-specific quests give large one-time EXP rewards and strengthen the character you'll rely on.
  4. Activate teleport points in every area you visit. Future you will be very grateful.
  5. Kill every Elite Boss in a region once (no stamina cost). Then use stamina on Flow Trial dungeons for steady daily EXP.
  6. Upgrade your Main DPS character first. Talent upgrades, weapon enhancement, and equipment all go to your primary damage dealer before anyone else.

Common Questions FAQ

Do I need to play the original Honor of Kings first? ▾
No. Honor of Kings: World is a completely standalone game with its own story, characters, and mechanics. Prior knowledge of the original MOBA is not required at all.
Is it pay-to-win? ▾
No. All content in the game is accessible to free-to-play players. Premium currency can be earned through regular gameplay, and microtransactions are mostly cosmetic or convenience-based.
How long does it take to finish the game? ▾
The main story takes approximately 40–60 hours. With side content, world exploration, and endgame systems, expect well over 100 hours of total play time.
Can I change my main character later? ▾
Yes. You can switch your main character and adjust your team composition at any point. Investment in characters transfers to their kit, so you are not permanently locked into your starter choice.
What's the best starting character for a total beginner? ▾
Lu Bu is the easiest for absolute beginners due to straightforward mechanics. Zhao Yun offers balanced gameplay for most players. Hou Yi is the best choice if you prefer keeping distance with ranged attacks.
