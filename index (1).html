IntroductionWhat is Honor of Kings: World?

Honor of Kings: World is an open-world action RPG — think of it like Genshin Impact but set in Eastern fantasy ancient China, with faster and more combat-focused gameplay. You explore a huge world called Primaera, fight enemies and massive bosses, build a team of three heroes, and uncover a story-driven adventure spanning 40–60 hours just for the main plot.

It was made by TiMi Studio Group (Tencent). The game shares characters with the original Honor of Kings mobile MOBA, but is completely standalone — no prior knowledge of that game is needed or helpful. Everything you need is explained here.

The game launched for PC in China in April 2026 and ran a global PC Alpha test May 21–27, 2026. A wider global release is expected later in 2026.

⚔️

Genre

Open-world Action RPG. Real-time combat with dodging, skill combos, and switching between heroes mid-fight.

🌏

World Size

A massive seamless open world with multiple distinct regions — forests, ruins, mountains, and underground zones.

👥

Solo or Co-op

The full main story can be completed entirely solo. Co-op with up to 3 other players is optional for bosses and dungeons.

🎮

Platform

Currently PC. Mobile version is planned. Cross-platform play is intended at full global launch.

💡 "Is this like Genshin Impact?" — Yes, structurally similar. You control one character at a time from a team of three, explore an open world, and fight bosses. The difference is faster, more action-focused combat and a Chinese imperial fantasy setting. If you have played Genshin, you will feel at home quickly. If you haven't, don't worry — this guide assumes zero experience.

Getting StartedWhat to Do in Your First Hour

When you first load in, it can feel overwhelming. Menus, systems, characters, quests, and a massive world all compete for your attention at once. Here is the exact order of what to focus on:

  1. Follow the tutorial and main story quest (Awakening Quests). The game hand-holds you through the basics in the first 20–30 minutes. Don't skip the dialogue — it introduces key characters and explains the world. Your story journal tracks this clearly.
  2. Learn to dodge before anything else. Press Shift or Right Click to dodge. Practice avoiding enemy attacks in the first few fights. This single mechanic will save your life more than any other in the entire game.
  3. Activate every teleport point you see. They appear as glowing beacons in the world. Walk up and interact. Once activated, you can fast-travel there from anywhere. Treat this as your first priority in every new zone you enter.
  4. Pick up everything off the ground. Wild herbs, food, and materials appear scattered everywhere. Grab them constantly. You need potions and food to heal during tough fights, and materials to upgrade characters later.
  5. Do your Daily Missions every day. Once they unlock (early in the story), Daily Missions are your single most efficient source of EXP and currency per minute played. Make them a habit every time you log in.
  6. Don't ignore Resonance Quests. These are quests tied to specific characters on your team. They appear in your journal and give large EXP rewards plus character-specific upgrades. Do them whenever they show up.
💡 The #1 beginner mistake: Getting distracted by the open world and ignoring the main story. Explore freely, but keep returning to story quests — they unlock critical systems, fast travel points, and new characters that make exploration far more enjoyable.

Core MechanicsHow Combat Works — Explained Simply

Combat is real-time — enemies move and attack while you do, and your timing matters. You control one character from your team of three, swapping between them during fights with Tab.

Controls at a glance:

Basic Controls

Left Click Basic attack
Hold Charged attack
Q E R Active skills
P Ultimate ability
Shift / Right Click Dodge
Tab Switch character

How Skills Charge

Skills Q E R are not free — they require Flow Energy built up by landing basic attacks. Think of basic attacks as fuel and skills as the fire. Attack consistently to keep your skills available.

The Perfect Dodge

Dodging at the exact moment an enemy attack lands triggers a Perfect Dodge — time slows briefly and opens an Enhanced Attack Window. Press E immediately after for a powerful follow-up hit.

The core loop of every fight:

① Attack

Spam basic attacks to generate Flow Energy. Watch the gauge fill on-screen.

② Use Skills

Fire Q, E, R as they charge. Each hit adds more energy, enabling more skills.

③ Dodge

Watch enemy animations. Dodge just before hits land to trigger the Perfect Dodge bonus.

④ Ultimate

Save P for key moments — especially when a boss is in Broken State (explained in Section 07).

🥷 Combat philosophy: "Dodge first, punish second." Never stand still trading hits. Always be moving. If you are in trouble, dodge away, let teammates absorb some damage, then re-engage.
🔼 Mid-air attacks: Attacking while jumping triggers different bonus effects depending on your character type. Experiment with your hero to find their aerial combos — they often deal extra damage.

InterfaceReading the HUD — What All Those Bars Mean

The health display confuses almost every new player. There are actually three separate health indicators on screen, not one:

Top bar
Your active (frontline) character's HP. Depletes when your current character takes hits.
Bottom bar
Your backline characters' combined HP. Characters not currently active take reduced damage.
Blue circle
CORE HP
The Source Flow Core — your team's true "life pool." You only actually lose when this reaches zero. Individual character deaths chip away at it, but it doesn't empty instantly.

In practice, your active character can die multiple times before you truly fail a fight. Each death reduces the Core HP. When Core HP hits zero — then it is over. This gives you much more room to recover from mistakes than it first appears.

💡 Low on HP? Press Tab to swap to another teammate. Your low-HP character moves to the backline where they take reduced damage, buying time to recover before swapping back.

Unique MechanicThe Primal Flow System — Your Avatar's Superpower

Here is what makes HoK: World unique: your main character (the Avatar, also called Origin Flow Child) is not locked to one fighting style. Using the Primal Flow System, your avatar shifts between four completely different weapon forms mid-combat.

Each weapon form changes your attack animations, skill set, and playstyle entirely. You switch by spending Flow Energy — the same energy generated by attacking. This keeps your avatar flexible and able to adapt to any situation.

⚪

Ring Blades

Fast spinning close-range attacks. High burst damage. Great for aggressive players who like mobile combat.

🏹

Lance

Long reach with thrusting strikes. Good for maintaining distance from dangerous enemies while still hitting hard.

🔨

Hammer

Heavy, slow swings with massive impact. Excellent for breaking enemy shields and staggering tough bosses.

⚔️

Sword

Balanced all-rounder. Good damage with decent speed. A reliable safe default for most situations — ideal for beginners.

✅ Season 1 upgrade: You can now assign two Flow Styles to your avatar and switch between them mid-fight. Previously you were locked to one. This significantly increases your combat flexibility and is a major improvement for new players.
💡 For beginners: Don't overthink weapon forms early on. Pick Sword or Ring Blades — both are forgiving. Stick with one while you learn the basics, then experiment once combat feels comfortable.

Combat DepthBoss Fights — How to Actually Win Them

Boss fights are where new players struggle first. Here is everything you need to know to stop dying and start winning.

  1. Red glow = dodge immediately. When a boss glows red, it is about to use a powerful attack. Stop attacking and dodge away right now. These hits can kill you in one or two strikes if you ignore the warning. Some skills and ultimates can interrupt these moves if timed correctly.
  2. Deplete the white Toughness bar. Every boss has a white bar below their main HP. This is their Toughness. Keep attacking — especially with Hammer form, which breaks toughness faster than other weapons. When the bar empties, the boss enters Broken State.
  3. Broken State = your big damage window. The boss is stunned. All damage you deal is increased. Press X for a powerful finisher move. Most importantly: use your Ultimate (P) right now. This is the correct moment to use it — not randomly during the fight.
  4. Boss shields block your moves. Some bosses deploy a shield mid-fight. While shielded, your red-glow skills are blocked. Focus on breaking the shield first (usually with specific skill types or heavy attacks) before worrying about dealing damage.
  5. Swap characters when you are in trouble. If your active character is near death, press Tab immediately. Never just stand there hoping to survive. Your team is there — use it.
⚠️ Most common beginner mistake: Using your Ultimate (P) as soon as it charges. Your Ultimate is most powerful during Broken State. Hold it until the boss is stunned, then unleash it for maximum damage.

Your TeamCharacters, Roles & Who to Pick First

You build a team of three characters alongside your Avatar. Each fills one of three roles. Think of it like a sports team — you need the right mix, not three of the same type. You switch between them mid-fight using Tab.

🛡 Frontline (Defender)

Stand in front, absorb damage, and keep enemies focused on them while your DPS attacks safely. They are tough and hard to kill — and they deal real damage too, not just soak hits.

  • Kaiser Beginner Friendly
  • Mulan Intermediate
  • Leng Chun Advanced
⚡ Main DPS (Damage Dealer)

Your primary attackers. They deal the most damage and determine how fast you clear bosses. They need protection — keep them behind your Frontline while they do their job.

  • Meng Ya Beginner Friendly
  • Garo Beginner Friendly
  • Navor Intermediate
✨ Support (Utility)

Not focused on damage. Instead they control the battlefield — slowing enemies, grouping them for your DPS, shielding allies, and boosting team stats. A good Support makes every fight noticeably easier.

  • Xi Shi Beginner Friendly
  • Jiang Ziya Intermediate

Best Picks for New Players

🛡

Kaiser — Best Starter Frontline

Activates an armoured state that reduces incoming damage and heals him through combat (lifesteal). Very forgiving — he keeps himself alive without requiring skill from the player.

🎯

Meng Ya — Best Starter DPS

A ranged gatling gun user who attacks from a safe distance. His "Anger Meter" builds into powerful suppression fire. Staying at range means fewer mistakes getting hit by close-range attacks.

✨

Xi Shi — Best Starter Support

Groups enemies together, interrupts their skills, and stabilises the whole battlefield. Her crowd control covers almost any team mistake. Every new player should start with her as Support.

🔓 How do you get more characters? Characters are unlocked by progressing through the main story, completing events, or purchasing them directly at a fixed price. There is no random gacha for characters — you always know exactly what you are working toward.

GrowthHow Leveling Works

HoK: World uses a dual-level system. It sounds complicated but is simple once understood.

Source Flow Level

Your main power level — like a character level in most RPGs. Unlocks character upgrades, weapon enhancements, and new game systems. This is what you are always trying to raise.

World Flow Level

The world's difficulty tier. Acts as a cap on your Source Flow Level — you cannot grow more powerful than the world allows. Raised by progressing through the main story.

EXP sources, ranked by how much to prioritise them:

Daily RoutineStamina & What to Do Each Day

Stamina is a resource that regenerates over time (roughly 160 units per day). It is spent on Flow Trial dungeons — the repeatable content that drops character upgrade materials. If you use it all at once, you wait for it to refill.

New players often burn all their stamina on day one without thinking. Here is the ideal daily routine:

  1. Complete all Daily Missions first. These reset every day and are your biggest source of EXP and currency for time spent. Takes about 20–30 minutes. Do these before anything else, every session.
  2. Spend your stamina on Flow Trial dungeons. Run them until your stamina is at a comfortable low. Don't hoard it — stamina has a cap, so unspent stamina above the cap is wasted. Spend it daily.
  3. Kill any Elite Bosses you haven't fought yet. First-time kills are free — no stamina cost, and they drop good EXP and materials. Always clear new Elite Bosses when you enter a new region.
  4. Push the main story if you have time. Even one quest per session keeps you moving forward. Story quests unlock new regions, systems, and characters.
  5. Collect resource nodes as you travel. Herbs, food, and materials respawn daily. Grab them while moving between objectives — they accumulate into hundreds of potions over time.
⚠️ Stamina cap warning: If you don't log in for several days and your stamina is already at the cap, any new regeneration is wasted. Log in daily even briefly to spend stamina and collect Daily Mission rewards.

Open WorldExploring Primaera — Tips That Save You Hours

HoK: World has no traditional minimap in the corner. Navigation uses a Directional Compass Line — a thin guiding line at the top of the screen pointing toward your objective. Follow the line. It feels unusual for the first 30 minutes, then becomes completely natural.

  1. Activate teleport points the moment you see them. Glowing beacons throughout the world. Interact to activate. Once active, you can teleport there from anywhere instantly. This is the single biggest quality-of-life upgrade in the game — never skip one.
  2. Pick up every resource node you pass. Glowing herbs, food, and material nodes appear everywhere and reset daily. Grab them while travelling — your potion supply depends on this habit.
  3. Do Kunyou instances when they appear. Special self-contained story scenarios marked on your compass. They reward high EXP and Resonance materials. Prioritise them over general wandering.
  4. Use the skateboard for fast travel. Your avatar can summon a skateboard for fast movement across flat terrain. Learn to switch to it quickly — it cuts travel time significantly and supports instant combat switching.
  5. Each region tracks exploration separately. The "Wandering Journey" interface shows your exploration percentage per zone. Each region has milestone rewards for completion. Follow the story first — don't try to 100% one area before moving on.

EconomyCurrency & What to Spend It On

Several currencies exist in HoK: World. Here is a plain explanation of each one and what it is actually for:

💰

Gameplay Currency

Earned freely from daily missions, quests, and events. Spend it on upgrade materials in the in-game shop. Focus purchases on your Main DPS character first.

💎

Jing Po (晶珀)

The premium currency. Earnable from gameplay achievements and events — or purchased with real money. Used for convenience items. Not required to progress through any content.

🎭

Heavenly Reward Banner

The only gacha-style mechanic — but it is cosmetic outfit skins only. Zero effect on gameplay or combat power. New players can safely ignore it entirely.

🎟️

Battle Pass

A seasonal pass rewarding upgrade materials and cosmetics as you play. The free tier gives solid rewards. The paid tier is optional — consider it only once you know you enjoy the game.

📌 Free-to-play verdict: All story content, all characters, all combat progression, and all endgame content is accessible without spending real money. Only cosmetic skins and Battle Pass premium tiers are behind paywalls. There is no pay-to-win element.

QuestionsFAQ — Things New Players Always Ask

Do I need to play the original Honor of Kings first? ▾
No, not at all. HoK: World is completely standalone with its own story, world, and characters. The only connection to the original MOBA is that some heroes appear in both games — but their stories and mechanics are entirely different here. Zero prior knowledge required or helpful.
Is there a gacha system? Can I lose characters to bad luck? ▾
No character gacha exists. All playable characters are earned through story, events, or purchased directly at a fixed price. There are no random pulls for characters or combat gear. The only gacha-style mechanic (Heavenly Reward) is for cosmetic outfit skins exclusively and has zero effect on gameplay strength.
I keep dying. What am I doing wrong? ▾
Almost always one of these four things: (1) Not dodging — press Shift or Right Click before enemy attacks land. (2) Ignoring red telegraphs — bosses glow red before big attacks; dodge immediately. (3) Not switching characters — when your active hero is low, press Tab to swap. (4) Wasting your Ultimate — save P for when the boss is in Broken State, not random moments during the fight.
What is the Primal Flow System? ▾
Your Avatar can switch between four weapon combat styles — Ring Blades, Lance, Hammer, and Sword — mid-fight by spending Flow Energy (generated by attacking). Each form completely changes your attack animations and skill set. In Season 1 you can have two styles active at once and switch between them freely during combat.
How do I get more characters? ▾
Characters are unlocked by: (1) progressing through the main story, (2) completing limited-time events, or (3) purchasing them directly at a fixed price. There are no random elements — you always know exactly what you are working toward and how to get it.
Do I need other players? Can I play solo? ▾
You can play the entire main story and most content completely solo. Multiplayer co-op (up to 4 players) is available for boss fights and dungeons but is entirely optional. The game is designed as a fully satisfying single-player experience.
How long is the game? ▾
The main story runs approximately 40–60 hours. Add side content, world exploration, character quests, and endgame systems, and expect well over 100 hours of total content. This is a substantial game — not a casual session experience.
What should I spend stamina on? ▾
Spend stamina on Flow Trial dungeons — they drop character upgrade materials. Use your ~160 daily stamina here rather than hoarding it, since stamina has a cap and unused regeneration above that cap is wasted. For bosses you have never fought: first kills are free (no stamina cost), so always clear new Elite Bosses before farming them with stamina.
When is the global release? ▾
The game launched on PC in China on April 10, 2026. A global PC Alpha test ran May 21–27, 2026. A mobile version is planned. The full global release date has not been officially announced as of May 2026. Follow the official Honor of Kings: World channels for updates.
